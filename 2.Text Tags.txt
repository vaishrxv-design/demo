Text content in HTML:
====================
* Based on the importance of heading, there are total six different heading tags are present in HTML.
        They are <h1>, <h2>, <h3>, <h4>, <h5>, <h6>
	By Default Heading Elements will be bold only.
	Based on the type of Heading the size of the Elements will be changed.
	<h1> denotes the Heading of Higher importance	
	<h6> denotes the Heading of least importance

* The content is represented by paragraph tag name called as <p>
	The main contents are represented by <p> tag name	
	Always the content inside the <p> tag starts from a new line
	<p>Paragraph</p>
        <p align="left">Aligns to the left</p>
        <p align="center">Aligns to the center</p>
        <p align="right">Aligns to the right</p>

* <center> - Used to align center of the webpage.

* <b> - To Bold a particular text <b>Bold</b>

* <i> - To make some text as italic. <i>italic</i>

* <sub> - To mention the subscript.  eg -->H2O-->water
	
* <sup> - To mention the superscript, Both are used to give mathematical formulas.   eg--> 2 power 3  = 2*2*2

* <br> - Line Break

* <hr> - Horizontal Rule, to give a single line for separation of text.
	 These are self closing tags which doesn't have content as well as closing tags.
         <hr size="50" width="50">
         <hr size="10" noshade>
         <hr size="10" color="Blue">

* <big> - <p><big>Larger text</big></p>
 
---> <small> - <p><small>Smaller text</small></p>

* <u> - <p>This is <u>Underline</u></p>

----> <strong> - To stress over a particular text. Text will be marked in the bold.

----> <em> - To emphasis the text which will give importance for a text. Text will be marked italic.
  Both are used to increase the meaning of the content Both the tags. 
  Both <b> and <i> are also does the same purpose as that of <strong> and <em>,
  but bold and italic doesn't have meaning. Strong and emp are semantic markup(it means meaningful content).  

----> <q> - To represent the Short Quotations.  

* <blackquote> - To define a section that is quoted from another source.

* <pre></pre> , <tt></tt>

----

* <abbr>	- Abbreviation 
format
     <p>The <abbr title="World Health Organization">WHO</abbr> was founded in 1948.</p>
     
* <acronym>	- Acronym     (this  not support in HTML5)
  The abbreviation tag contains title attribute which will give full form of the abbreviation. 
  When the mouse cursor is moved over the abbreviation then it will show the full content.
  The first letter of each word represents the acronym. The same title applies for Acronym tag also.

* <cite> - Citation - It defines title of a creative work(Eg. A book, A poem, A movie, A painting, A sculpture).
           The text inside the cite tag will be italic.
          <p>The Scream by<cite>Edvard Munch</cite>. Painted in 1893.</p>

* <dfn> - Definition
	Gives the Definiton of a word.
	The content inside the cite tag will be italic.
	The definition tag is used to define a terminology.
     <p><dfn>HTML</dfn> is the standard markup language for creating web pages.</p>

* <address> - Generally to mention for search engines that this is the address we use address tag.
	
* <del> - Delete
  <p>After picking up some groceries, I went <del>home</del> back to my apartment.</p>


* <ins> - insert
  <p>After picking up some groceries, I went <del>home</del> <ins>back to my apartment</ins>.</p>                                      


* <s>	- strikedout
	The S and STRIKE elements defines strikethrough text. 
        The use of these elements is deprecated. 
  <p>Normal text, <s>Strikethrough text,</s> <strike>Strikethrough text,</strike></p>

   <!-- This is comment line -->
